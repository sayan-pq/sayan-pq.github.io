    About Me

    Welcome!

    I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematics, Vanderbilt University.

    I am broadly interested in probability theory and its application to statistical physics, combinatorics, and graph theory. See Research page for details.

    I was a Dickson Instructor in University of Chicago from 2023 to 2026. I received my PhD in Mathematics from Columbia University in 2023. My advisor was Ivan Corwin.

    Beside probability, I like chess, city photography, drone flying, and anime.
